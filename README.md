<h1 align="center">Shortlist</h1>

Shortlist tailors your CV and writes a cover letter for each job you apply to, for engineers in every field, from civil and electrical to software and chemical. It runs on your own Windows PC and works offline. Your CV never leaves your computer, and no AI service is involved.

## How it works

1. **Import your master CV** once: the long version with everything you have done, as a PDF, a Word file (.docx) or LaTeX (.tex).
2. **Paste a job posting.** Shortlist reads it, flags the visa clause, the degree asked for and the locations, and shows which of the posting's skills your CV proves, only lists, or is missing.
3. **Review and save.** Shortlist picks the bullets, modules and skills that best match the job, writes a cover letter, and shows both side by side. Click any part to edit it, approve each document, then save. Each job gets its own folder with the CV and cover letter as PDF and LaTeX.

## What it will not do

Shortlist never adds a skill, module or piece of experience you have not written. It chooses and orders what is already in your master CV, and tells you what the posting asks for that your CV does not show.

## Features

- UK or US spelling, switched with one click
- LinkedIn or portfolio link in the CV header, chosen per job
- A 10-second recruiter check on every CV: clear target, the job's language, outcomes, scale, progression
- Warnings for bullets with no figure or no stated result
- Job numbers and reference IDs never appear in a cover letter
- Reopen a saved application later to edit and resave it
- Light and dark mode

## Privacy

Shortlist makes no network requests. Your profile and settings stay in `%APPDATA%\Shortlist` on your PC, and your documents go to the folder you choose (`Documents\Shortlist` by default).
