How it works

1

Import your master CV

Bring in your full CV once, with every role, project and bullet. Shortlist checks it and remembers your contact details.

2

Paste a job posting

It reads what the job asks for, and flags right-to-work and degree requirements. It also shows which skills you can prove, which you only list and which are missing.

3

Review, edit and save

See a two-page CV and a one-page cover letter. Swap or reword any bullet, approve each document, and save the PDFs with their LaTeX source.

Your CV never leaves your computer.

Shortlist works fully offline. There is no account, no upload and no cloud. Everything it reads and writes stays in your Documents folder.

It only uses experience you have written. If the job asks for a skill you don't have, Shortlist tells you rather than claiming it.
